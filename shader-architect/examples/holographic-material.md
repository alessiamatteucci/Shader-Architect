# Holographic material

Design recipe, not a compiled or visually verified shader.

## Create brief

```text
/CREATE
Kind: fill
Create a pearlescent foil surface with restrained spectral shifts tied to a directional surface field. Preserve a clear light hierarchy and avoid a uniform full-frame rainbow.
Controls: Base color, spectral strength, band scale, orientation, grain amount.
```

## Compatibility and quality

This is an artistic spectral model unless physical interference is actually implemented.

## Targeted refinement

```text
/REFINE [actual shader ID]
Reduce saturation while preserving band spacing, grain and orientation.
```
